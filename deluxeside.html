SEO Case Study · DeluxeSide

Growing a large fashion store through search-focused e-commerce SEO.

DeluxeSide operates across multiple fashion categories, from sneakers and bags to clothing, accessories, and seasonal collections. The SEO work focused on improving category relevance, commercial search targeting, internal structure, and visibility across international markets.

The challenges

A large e-commerce catalog creates more SEO opportunities — and more complexity.

DeluxeSide already had a broad product range and active demand, but continued growth required clearer keyword targeting, stronger category relevance, and a more organized search structure.

01

Highly competitive e-commerce keywords

Sneakers, luxury fashion, bags, clothes, and accessories are crowded search categories. Competing effectively requires strong commercial relevance across many pages.

02

Large and diverse product catalog

With sneakers, bags, high heels, slippers, boots, jewellery, belts, sunglasses, accessories, and more, every category needed a clear place in the website’s search structure.

03

Category and product pages needed continued optimization

Several commercial pages were already receiving strong traffic, showing clear demand but also highlighting the need to keep improving search relevance and landing-page quality.

04

International search demand

Visitors were arriving from multiple countries, so the SEO strategy needed to support broader international growth rather than focusing on only one local market.

What we did

Built the SEO strategy around commercial intent, category relevance, and site structure.

The work focused on helping search engines understand the store more clearly while directing users toward the categories and products with the strongest commercial value.

✓

E-commerce keyword optimization

Core categories such as sneakers, designer sneakers, bags, women’s fashion, clothes, boots, high heels, and accessories were aligned with commercially relevant search intent.

✓

Category and product page optimization

Titles, meta descriptions, headings, product copy, category relevance, and page structure were improved to strengthen search relevance.

✓

Internal linking and site architecture

Connections between categories, subcategories, products, and related pages were strengthened so both users and search engines could navigate the store more clearly.

✓

Commercial search optimization

Pages tied directly to sneaker orders, bags, clothing, sale products, fashion products, and online shopping were prioritized as key landing pages.

Strong search visibility and high user activity across commercial pages.
